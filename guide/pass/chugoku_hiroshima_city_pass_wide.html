Hiroshima City Pass Wide (広島シティパスワイド)

ผู้ให้บริการ: Hiroden with seven bus operators (MOBIRY DAYS commuter-style pass)

None

ข้อจำกัด / ใช้ไม่ได้

Hiroden between 修大協創中高前 and 広電宮島口 excluded; highway bus (Hiroshima-Hamada), night bus, Clear Line, Meipuru-pu, Tsubaki Bus, AEON Mall shuttle excluded. Crossing zone boundary charges extra section fare.

ซื้อที่ไหน

Not stated on fetched text; sold as an MOBIRY DAYS commuter pass; sales open two weeks before start date. Separate purchase guides exist.
