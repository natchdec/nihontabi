None

ระยะเวลาใช้งาน

28 days, including the date of purchase; card becomes invalid on the 29th day. The GOOD THRU note on the back is not the expiry date.

No refunds regardless of validity status or remaining balance. Top-up max 20,000 yen at Automatic Ticket Vending Machines and Fare Adjustment Machines; top-up only in yen (credit card not accepted for top-up). Only one Welcome Suica per individual in principle.
