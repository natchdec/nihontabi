Hakone Freepass

ผู้ให้บริการ: Odakyu Electric Railway (with Hakone Tozan and partner operators) · เมืองฐานที่เกี่ยวข้อง: นาโกย่า, โตเกียว

ระยะเวลาใช้งาน

2 or 3 days, starting on the purchase date regardless of time of purchase. A 2-day pass ends after the last service on the second day (not 48 hours).

ราคา (เยน)

รายการผู้ใหญ่เด็ก
2-day, from Shinjuku¥7,100¥1,600
3-day, from Shinjuku¥7,500¥1,850
2-day, from Odawara (Hakone Area Only)¥6,000¥1,500
3-day, from Odawara (Hakone Area Only)¥6,400¥1,750

ข้อจำกัด / ใช้ไม่ได้

Romancecar limited express is not included (reserved seat extra: Shinjuku to Hakone-Yumoto +1,200 yen adult / +600 yen child, bought separately; 50 yen cheaper online). Children under 6 need no ticket. Odawara-to-Hakone-Yumoto reserved seats sold only at platform when seats remain.
