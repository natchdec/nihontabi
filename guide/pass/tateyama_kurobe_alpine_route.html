Tateyama Kurobe Alpine Route ticket

ผู้ให้บริการ: Tateyama Kurobe Alpine Route (Toyama Chihō Railway / Kurobe Dam operators)

None

ระยะเวลาใช้งาน

Same-day tickets valid only on purchase day. Web tickets on one-way sections valid 5 days (snippet).

ข้อจำกัด / ใช้ไม่ได้

Child 6-11 pays half; under 6 free. Same-day tickets cannot be bought in advance. Early-bird fares require advance purchase.

ซื้อที่ไหน

Official WEB ticket site (alpen-route.com); same-day tickets at Tateyama and Dentetsu Toyama stations.
